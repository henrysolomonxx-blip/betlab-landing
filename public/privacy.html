Privacy

This page covers the BetLab early-access page at betlab.us. The BetLab app has its own terms and privacy notice.

What we collect

When you sign up for early access we save three things: the email address you enter, which of the two forms on the page you used, and the date and time. We don't ask for anything else.

What we use it for

To email you about BetLab's launch and early access. Nothing else.

Who sees it

We don't sell your email address or share it for anyone else's marketing. The list is stored with Cloudflare, the company that hosts this page. If we use an email service to send the launch email, your address is passed to that service for that purpose only.

Cookies and tracking

This page has no advertising trackers and sets no tracking cookies. After you sign up, your browser remembers that you did, so the form isn't shown to you again. The page's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and Cloudflare keeps standard technical logs of requests to the site.

Getting off the list

Every email we send will have an unsubscribe link. You can also write to henrysolomon@betlab.us at any time and we'll delete your address.

Age

BetLab is for adults 21 and over. Please don't sign up if you're younger.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

How it works

Three steps to a rank.

  1. 01

    Start free with Pick'em

    No sportsbook needed. Make up to five rated picks a day against real lines and climb from Bronze as far as Gold.

  2. 02

    Link your sportsbook

    Connect it once and your slips import on their own. Nobody types in a bet by hand, so nobody can invent a record.

  3. 03

    Climb the Verified ladder

    Rank up to five bets a day. Each one is graded against the odds you took, so picking well moves you up and betting more doesn't.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is BetLab a sportsbook?

No. BetLab takes no bets, holds no money and pays nothing out. It ranks how well you pick.

What does it cost?

It's free at launch, and that includes the Verified ladder.

Do I need a sportsbook account?

No. Pick'em is free, needs nothing linked, and takes you as far as Gold. Platinum and above are earned with real bets from a linked sportsbook.

What can BetLab see when I link a sportsbook?

It reads your bet slips so it can grade them. It never shows stakes, payouts or balances, and only the bets you choose to rank count toward your rank or appear on your profile.

How is my rank worked out?

From the odds and the result of each ranked bet. A win at long odds moves you more than a win on a heavy favourite, and how much you staked never matters. You can rank up to five bets a day.

When does it open?

Season 1 doesn't have a date yet. Everyone on the early-access list gets an email when it opens.
